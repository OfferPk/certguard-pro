\# CertGuard Pro — Commercialization & Growth Playbook (v1.0.0)

\#\# 1\. Market Opportunity & Value Proposition

Every domain connecting to banking, payment gateways, healthcare patient portals, and mission-critical SaaS relies on SSL/TLS certificates. 

Yet, over \*\*75% of top 100,000 domains either lack CAA records or configure them incorrectly\*\*, allowing any compromised CA globally to issue forged certificates.

CertGuard Pro provides the definitive toolset to model, audit, and deploy bulletproof CAA records with zero infrastructure friction.
